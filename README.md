## **Community Services**

During my internship period, I participated in multiple community-oriented activities to contribute locally alongside my technical learning. These initiatives covered healthcare support through voluntary blood donation, environmental care through sapling plantation, and assisting senior citizens in my local area.

### **Activities Involved**

- **Blood Donation** – Donated a unit of blood at Aaraadhya Blood Center, Kukatpally, Hyderabad, Telangana, to support patients in need of emergency transfusions.
  
- **Tree Plantation Drive** – Planted a total of 4 tree saplings across two locations: 1 sapling in front of my residence in Mallampet, Hyderabad, and 3 saplings on family property in Bollaram (near Miyapur), Hyderabad.

- **Helping Elder Citizens** – Visited 4 elderly individuals in my neighborhood and helped them with essential household necessities, including bringing fresh fruits, groceries, and daily food items.

### **Impact / Contribution**

- Supported local blood bank reserves at Aaraadhya Blood Center, Kukatpally, directly helping emergency care availability.
- Contributed to local green cover by planting and caring for 4 saplings across Mallampet and Bollaram.
- Provided direct, practical support to senior neighbors by taking care of everyday food and supply errands.
- Developed a stronger sense of social responsibility and community connection outside of technical work.
